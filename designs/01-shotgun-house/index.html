    What this is

    AI Friday is a meetup and a Slack. Once a month we take over a room, four or five people show something they made or something they finally figured out, and then everybody talks for an hour.

    The people who come are lawyers, teachers, nurses, bookkeepers, people who run small businesses. They use a computer all day. They are not engineers.

    You don't have to be technical. That is the entire point.

    What people are building

    • Intake, before the first call

      A family lawyer · Mid-City

      A new client fills out a form. She gets a plain summary and three questions to ask, before she picks up the phone.

    • A week of science on Sunday night

      Seventh grade teacher · Algiers

      She pastes in the state standards. It gives back five days of labs and worksheets she can actually run on Monday.

    • The schedule nobody had to redo

      Restaurant owner · Freret Street

      Everybody texts him when they can't work. It reads the texts and builds the week around them.

    • Every deadline in one place

      Program manager · Central City

      Grant PDFs go in. Dates come out on a calendar she checks every Monday morning.

    What you'll leave with

    1. One thing you can use Monday

      Somebody always demos something small enough to copy down and try at your own desk the next morning.

    2. Three people who do your job

      We do introductions at the start, out loud, by what you do. You will meet people in your line of work.

    3. A clear sense of what AI can't do

      Worth as much as knowing what it's good at. It saves you three weeks on the wrong idea.

    Everything else
    happens in the kitchen

    Between meetups it's a Slack. People post what they're stuck on before they post what worked, and somebody usually answers within the hour.

    Nobody is made to feel stupid for asking a basic question, because most of us are asking basic questions.
