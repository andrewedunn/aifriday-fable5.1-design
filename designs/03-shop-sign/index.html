Free. Come by yourself, it's fine.

AI Friday is a room in New Orleans where people show each other what they've made with AI. It meets the first Friday of the month. Between meetups it lives in a Slack.

Most of us are not developers. There are lawyers here, teachers, nurses, people who run one small business by themselves. The demos are short, the questions are basic on purpose, and nobody is selling anything.

Four things members made this year.

  • Client intake bot 2 hrs a week

    Kara, immigration lawyer

    Reads new inquiry emails and drafts the first reply. She still sends every one by hand, but she isn't starting from a blank page.

  • Lesson builder Sunday night back

    Marcus, seventh grade science

    Turns a state standard into a worksheet, a warm-up, and three questions for the kids who finish early.

  • Shift filler 20 min a shift

    Dana, restaurant general manager

    When somebody calls out, it texts the three people most likely to say yes instead of the whole staff group chat.

  • Grant sifter 6 hrs a month

    Priya, nonprofit director

    Reads every funder announcement that lands in her inbox and flags the two that are actually worth an afternoon.

  • Three things to try Monday

    Every demo comes with the actual prompt and the actual setup. You leave with notes, not a feeling.

  • One person to ask

    You'll meet somebody doing your job in a different building. That is most of what a room is for.

  • A start on your own thing

    Bring one task you hate. We'll get a rough first version of it working before you leave.

Between meetups it's a Slack. People post what they tried, what broke, and what finally worked. It's slow enough to keep up with.
