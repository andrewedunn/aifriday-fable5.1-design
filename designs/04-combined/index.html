What this is

AI Friday is a meetup in New Orleans. Once a month, on a Friday, after work.

People bring something they made with AI and show it to the room. Most of it is half finished. That's the part worth seeing.

Nobody is selling anything. You don't have to bring anything your first time. Come look.

What people made

  • The intake bot

    Renée · family law

    Takes the first call from a new client, asks what she would have asked, and has the file started before Monday.

  • Monday through Friday

    Marcus · teaches eighth grade science

    He feeds it one standard and gets back a week of worksheets he actually wants to hand out.

  • The schedule

  • The RFP reader

What you leave with

  • One thing you can use Monday

    Someone will show you a prompt, a tool, or a shortcut. Write it down. It will work.

  • Two or three names

    People with your job, in your city, stuck on the same thing. Now you can text them.

  • Proof it isn't that hard

    Someone who is not a programmer already built the thing you assumed needed one.

The kitchen

Every party ends up in the kitchen. Ours is a Slack.

People post what they're stuck on, what finally worked, and what they're bringing Friday. It moves slowly enough to keep up with.
